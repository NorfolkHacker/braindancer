NAVIGATION · SIX RULES

1 · SELECT WALKS THE PAGES

Every page of the section in one chain, a page a detent, stopping at both ends. No levels, no zoom.

FX: SENDS → SPACE → MASTER → MORE

2 · THE BUTTON: IN, OR ON

From another section: the page you were last on there. Already inside: the next page (round).

SEQ › STEPS: SEQ → STEP EXTRAS · GLO › LIVE: GLO → MIX

3 · PRESETS LOADS PRESETS

Always: the track's sounds; SEQ the patterns (PHRASES, OCT+ loads); GEN the recent pieces. Never a page, a track or a module.

FX › SPACE: PRESETS ▶ the next sound

4 · KNOB 1 PICKS

The step, the slot, the stage, the operator, the kit's sound, the row. The track: always ALGORITHM.

SEQ › STEPS: K1 = all 64 steps · MORE: K1 = the row, K2 its value

5 · HOLD = THE LAYER

Held + SELECT: HOME GEN · EDIT SEQ LFO FX their dice · GLO the tempo · ENV SCL ARP SAVE their chain.

SEQ + G5 CLR STEP · SAVE + C5 NAME

6 · THE SCREEN SAYS WHERE

The header names the section and the track; the status line what the knobs and the next tap do. Footer: the page's place, n / N.

THE PANEL

KNOBS AND TRANSPORT

MASTERMaster volume.
SELECTWalks the section's pages, a page a detent, stopping at the ends (the MENU: its pages). Held with a button: HOME GEN; EDIT · SEQ · LFO · FX their dice (OCT+ keeps, OCT− back); GLO the tempo; ENV · SCL · ARP · SAVE their chain.
PRESETSAlways presets: the sounds (factory and user) on every page; SEQ: the patterns (PHRASES); GEN: the recent pieces. Nothing in the MENU or with a button held.
ALGORITHMSelects the track from any page: T1–T6 (T1–T4 with TRACKS 4). MENU: nothing. A layer held: MAP · KNOBS · STATE.
KNOB 1–4The four knobs on screen, in their colours; KNOB 1 picks (step, slot, row). Fast sweeps the whole range, slow fine-tunes. With a layer held: the layer's. A step held: a lock on that step.
OCT− · OCT+The keyboard's octave (both together: back to 0); on DRUM the kit stays put: +1/+2/+3 REPEAT 1/2, REPEAT, RATCHET X2; −1 SHORT, −2 GHOST, −3 FLAM. On action rows and dialogs: back / confirm.
PLAYStart / stop. GLO + PLAY: restart from the top.
RECArms live recording on the selected track (notes and knob moves, overdub). On SEQ › STEPS stopped: step recording.
HOMETap: HOME (on HOME the next page: TRACKS · VISUAL · GEN). Hold: the MENU.
KEYS27 keys play the selected track; with a layer held they become that layer's; on GLO › LIVE they are the tricks.

MIDI PER TRACK · THE CLOCK

KNOB 1 IN, KNOB 2 OUT, KNOB 3 CHANNEL (1–16) of the selected track (ALGORITHM picks it; its row lit in the table), KNOB 4 CLOCK INT / USB / TRS. A track plays the notes and CCs of its channel while IN is on, and sends its notes on its channel over USB while OUT and NOTES OUT are on. Channel 16's Program Change always picks a section or project. GLO › MORE › MIDI: KEYBOARD PLAYS SELECTED (notes on a channel no track listens to play the selected track), CLOCK OUT (USB only: the TRS socket is an input), NOTES OUT OFF / KEYS / KEYS+SEQ, LEARN, LEARN CLEAR. Under CLOCK USB or TRS the tempo is the clock's.
