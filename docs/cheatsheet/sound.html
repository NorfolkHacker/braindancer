DICE · GEN

EDIT + SELECT: a random factory preset of the engine, every setting moved up to a quarter of its range (DRUM-X: both sides of every sound around the factory kit). Heard at once: OCT+ keeps it, OCT− brings the sound back. SEQ / LFO / FX + SELECT: the sequence, the LFO, the effects, the same way. HOME + SELECT: GEN, HOME's last page: ENERGY · FORM (LOOP, PARTS, SONG) · COMPLEX · TAPE (AUTO, OFF, 10–100). OCT+ GENERATE picks a style itself (sometimes WILD, often a blend: RETROBOC+AMBIENT 0421); PRESETS steps back through the last 8 pieces and OCT+ RECALL brings one back. OCT− back to TRACKS. GLO + G4 undoes.

STEPS · MACROS

LFO › MORE › STEPS · A 16-LEVEL SEQUENCER

LENGTH, STEP RATE, SMOOTH, STEP 1–16, in time with the transport. Use it from MOD MATRIX, SOURCE STEPS.

MACROS M1–M4

Hold LFO: the knobs are M1–M4, latched until LFO again (also HOME › VISUAL). F3 rolls new routes, G3 clears them. By hand: MOD MATRIX, SOURCE MACRO 1–4. Saved with the project.

SEQUENCER

64 steps a track, up to 4 notes a step. Four pages: STEPS (the roll; on DRUM the lane grid; KNOB 1 all 64 steps, SELECT on the last cell the next 16), STEP EXTRAS (the picked step's RATCHET, CHANCE, CONDITION EVERY 2ND / 1ST OF 4 …, MICRO in 64ths), PATTERN, MORE. Hold a step + turn a knob: a lock (LOCK · SETTING · VALUE). Step recording: stopped, REC on STEPS; a chord held + KNOB 1 its length, + ARP arpeggiated. MORE › STEP: two components a step (CHANCE, RATCHET, ARP, SEQ, WALK, PULSE, OCTAVE, RANDOM, BEND, TONAL, JUMP, REVERSE, CONDITION, FILL, KILL) and EVERY. TRACK: SEQ PITCH, CHANCE thins the whole track, HUMAN, REC SNAP. SHAPE: STRETCH, REPEAT, NUDGE, ROTATE as you turn. REC + knobs while playing: automation (64 records a section; MORE › AUTOMATION plays, clears and lists them).

SCALE · ARP

ROOT and SCALE are every melodic track's: CHROMATIC MAJOR MINOR DORIAN MIXOLYDIAN PENTATONIC MINOR PENT HARMONIC MINOR PHRYGIAN LYDIAN LOCRIAN MELODIC MINOR BLUES WHOLE TONE and two DIMINISHED. SNAP puts the keys (or the sequence) on the scale, ALL TRACKS moves every sequence along it. CHORD: a key plays a chord (TRIAD 7TH ADD9 SUS2 SUS4 in key, MAJ MIN 7 MAJ7 MIN7 POWER), VOICING LEAD leads each to the next, STRUM as a harp.

SONGS

4 projects × 4 sections (A–D), a song of up to 16 rows by bars (SAVE › SONG, OCT+ adds a row, PLAY plays; END: STOP, x2–x8, LOOP), SLOOP style. Each row can hold a scene: mutes, DRUM-X group mutes, macros and punch-ins. SAVE held plays sections live on the next bar, stores them, records the song (SONG REC). Each section keeps its sounds, steps, automation, kit and its SLICER.

MASTER

CLIPPUNCHDUCKAGE

CLIP saturates without getting louder. PUNCH drives the drums: denser body. DUCK: the kick pulls the rest down (DUCK TIME, its release, is in FX › MORE › MASTER). AGE: old tape in one knob, setting TAPE's WOW, FLUTTER, TAPE SAT, HISS and TAPE TONE together (off at 0; the details are in FX › MORE › TAPE). An always-on leveler before the limiter keeps patches at a steady volume. DJ FILTER (GLO › LIVE KNOB 4): left a low-pass, right a high-pass.

PUNCH-IN FX (FX HELD)

REPEAT 1/8 · 1/16 · 1/32, GLITCH, TAPE STOP, FREEZE, SHUFFLE; on the notes: OCT−/+, ½ TEMPO, DECAY −/+, STUTTER, ATTACK +, ARP, RANDOM; sweeps CRUSH THROW. Knobs: STEP LOOP, INTERVAL, MUTATE, DJ FILTER. Recordable per section (FX › MORE › PUNCH LANE).
