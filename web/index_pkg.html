    Braindancer manual
    
  
    Braindancer builds on open firmware for the FM-1: JIANT FM by Juan Jiant (juanjiant-bit.github.io/JIANT) and Felucca by Hügelton Instruments. To support JIANT's author: Ko-fi (in Argentina: Cafecito).
    
  
    Credits: Felucca by Leo Kuroshita (@kurogedelic), Hügelton Instruments, with its CrispyZebra waveforms and Fukiai icons (MIT); the song system after SLOOP by isod89.
    
    Font: Inter Tight (The Inter Project Authors, SIL OFL 1.1).
    
    FM6 engine: msfa from Dexed (Google Inc., Pascal Gauthier, Apache-2.0). The package also holds three JieLi AC79 SDK files, uboot.boot, cfg_tool.bin and eq_cfg_hw.bin (JieLi Technology, Apache-2.0). Licences: GPL-3.0, Apache-2.0, all.
