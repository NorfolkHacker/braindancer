ACID FM

IDM / braindance firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: virtual analog, FM, chopped breaks, songs that write themselves.

InstallChrome or Edge, FM-1 on USB, about a minute Try itthe same firmware in your browser, with sound Manualevery page, knob and button

Where it is

  1. Pipeline: 0.1, the build proven to install and roll back
  2. Trim: engines chosen for IDM, the voice ceiling measured, room made for samples
  3. The ACID screen theme: 3a in 0.3 (new look, HOME, splash); 3b in 0.4 (the sound sections, IDM genres in GEN, a power-on demo); 3c next
  4. BREAK: load breakbeats from this site, chop and rearrange them on the device
  5. Sound: TAPE wow and flutter, 303-style accent and slide, new FM and drum voices
  6. The song generator: ACID, BRAINDANCE, DRILL'N'BASS, RETROBOC, AMBIENT, GLITCH

Before you install

At your own risk. Back up the full flash first with MvaveFM1Unbricker. The FM-1's ROM bootloader cannot be overwritten: a failed install can be rescued, and the official firmware can always go back on.
